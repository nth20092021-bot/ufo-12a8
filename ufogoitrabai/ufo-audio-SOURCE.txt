Peaceful scene music and pet sounds — added 2026-10-15
Original procedural audio authored for this game in sound.js. No external recording or downloaded music is used. The calm score is a 24-second phrase at 80 BPM with soft triangle/sine plucks, rests, and sparse two-note harmonies. On visible page load, the game creates an audio context without forcing a resume and starts music if browser autoplay policy permits. A clear enable-sound button remains when blocked; a genuine click or key resumes the context. A visible character bubble is requested as soon as idle audio is ready. Speech autoplay denial has a separate enable-voices prompt. Music ducks during character/welcome speech and stops outside the visible idle scene. The result overlay permits the idle score and character dialogue only after all result recordings, the preserved siren tail, and any pending or active result speech finish; a new result reading pauses ambience again.
Dog double barks and cat meows are short synthesized effects, using filtered noise and changing pitched/formant tones. They accompany occasional decorative pet visits, never alter gameplay, and yield to spoken dialogue/welcomes. These generated effects are not recordings of real animals.

Character dialogue voices — expanded 2026-10-15
The 80 authored idle phrases are translated into all nine interface languages. Browser speech uses the matching interface language and the character gender profile. Known matching male/female platform voices are selected when both exist. A single compatible voice uses pitch/rate contrast (male0.85/0.93; female1.12/0.98); no separate male engine is claimed when unavailable. The current local Vietnamese inventory has Linh only. Existing result and welcome voice defaults remain unchanged.
